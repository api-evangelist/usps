WILLIAMSTOWN, MA — The Postal Service has released a new Christmas stamp featuring a depiction of the Madonna and Child from a painting in the collection of the Sterling and Francine Clark Art Institute in Williamstown, MA. The stamp was issued today without a ceremony.
